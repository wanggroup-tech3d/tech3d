Technology-Driven Drug Discovery (Tech3D) Group

Our work brings together three cutting-edge technologies:

Computational biology — using powerful algorithms to predict how well a drug will work before it is even made.

Molecular libraries — allowing us to test billions of potential drug candidates at once.

Nanotechnology — creating tiny reaction droplets that help us quickly see which drugs are effective.
